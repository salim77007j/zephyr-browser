Protection layers

How Zephyr protects you

Host-level blocking — navigations and requests to known advertising, tracking and malware hosts are cancelled inside the engine before any connection is made.
Script-level filtering — fetch, XMLHttpRequest, sendBeacon and element loads are intercepted inside every page and matched against the compiled block data.
Element-level hiding — cosmetic rules from EasyList remove the empty ad frames left behind.
Fingerprint protection — canvas, audio and WebGL readbacks are noised with a per-session deterministic seed; navigator signals are clamped.
